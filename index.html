ABOUT US

SPACE EDGE CONSULTANTS is an ISO 9001:2015 certified multi-disciplinary Architectural Design & Engineering Consultancy firm with main expertise in Architecture, Urban Planning, Master Planning, Site Planning, Engineering and Contract Management. We have also branched out to include Project Feasibility, Planning & Development Studies including Project Management and Environmental Design. ​

Founded in year 2015 by Principal Architect & Urban Planner, Sumit Tripathi, a diverse group that tackles designs with a different approach to every project from basic architectural stages including Conceptual Architectural Design, Schematic Design, Design Development, Contract Development, and Construction Implementation

Over the years, we have carried out the architecture, master planning, and urban design of Multi-modal Transport Terminals, Retail and Entertainment centers, Commercial, Office, Institutional, Industrial, Residential, and Tourism Estates, and Mixed-Use Development projects of buildings

Space Edge Consultants has been pioneering in providing strategic consultancy services to leading Authorities, Developers, and Contractors.
