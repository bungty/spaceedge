WHO WE ARE

ABOUT SPACE EDGE CONSULTANTS

SPACE EDGE CONSULTANTS is an ISO 9001:2015 certified multi-disciplinary Architectural Design & Engineering Consultancy firm with core expertise in Architecture, Urban Planning, Master Planning, Site Planning, Engineering and Contract Management.

Founded in 2015 by Principal Architect & Urban Planner Sumit Tripathi, the firm approaches every project with a contextual and research-driven mindset — from conceptual design to construction implementation.

Over the years, we have delivered multi-modal transport terminals, commercial and institutional buildings, residential and tourism estates, and large-scale mixed-use developments across India.

We continue to pioneer strategic consultancy services for leading authorities, developers, and contractors.

Design That Endures Time

Our ideal is to design in such a reduced form, that the results endure content and time. Formal restriction and uniformity of materials contribute to this standpoint because we understand obviousness as a categorical imperative.
